Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2025

At KothaTales ("we", "our", "us"), your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use the KothaTales mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information depending on your use of the App:

a. Personal Information (if you sign in)

  • Email address
  • Google account basic profile (if signing in with Google)
  • Authentication token and user ID (from Firebase Authentication)

b. Usage and Gameplay Data

  • Story progress (last read chapter, completed stories)
  • Unlocked chapters and premium content
  • Diamond balance and Reader XP
  • Bonus reward claims (welcome, daily, events)

c. Device & Technical Information

  • Device type, OS version, app version
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • Anonymous usage data collected via Firebase Analytics

d. Guest Users

If you use the App without logging in (guest mode), your progress is stored only locally on your device. This data is not synced to any server and may be lost if the app is deleted or the device is reset.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide core app functionality (e.g., reading and unlocking stories)
  • To process in-app purchases securely via Google Play Billing
  • To manage rewards, Reader XP, and Diamond bonuses
  • To personalize and improve your user experience
  • To monitor app performance and diagnose technical issues
  • To ensure secure authentication and data storage

3. How We Store and Protect Your Information

  • User account data is securely stored in Firebase Realtime Database or Firestore.
  • Authentication is handled via Firebase Authentication with secure token exchange.
  • All communications with our backend servers and Firebase are encrypted using HTTPS and follow industry best practices.

4. Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to collect aggregated, anonymized data such as app usage patterns, feature engagement, and crash reports. This helps us improve the performance and usability of the App.

5. In-App Purchases

All in-app purchases are processed securely via the Google Play Billing Library. We do not collect or store your credit card or billing information. Purchase tokens are verified securely using backend APIs.

6. Bonus Rewards and A/B Testing

Some users may receive special bonuses (e.g., welcome, daily, event-based) or be part of A/B testing experiments. These variations are assigned programmatically and may affect app experience or bonus eligibility.

7. Data Retention

We retain user data as long as the account remains active or as necessary to provide services. If you choose to delete your account, your data will be permanently removed from our systems (subject to verification and processing time).

8. Account Deletion

Users can request account deletion by contacting our support team. Deletion requests are handled manually and may take several business days. Once processed, all user-associated data will be permanently deleted from Firebase servers.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

10. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share data only when:

  • Required by law or legal obligation
  • Necessary to enforce our Terms and Conditions
  • Needed to protect our rights, property, or safety

11. Third-Party Services

KothaTales uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google)
  • Google Play Billing

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the App or on our website. Continued use of the App after any updates indicates your acceptance of the new terms.
