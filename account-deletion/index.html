Request Account Deletion

Note: Account deletion is a manual process and may take a few business days to complete. We appreciate your patience.

If you want to delete your KothaTales account, you can:

  • Request deletion from the KothaTales app via Profile & Settings → Delete Account
  • Or send an email request from your registered email address

Send your email to:
